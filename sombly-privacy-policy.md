## Information Collected and Used by Sombly

Sombly may receive, collect, store, and process information when you
download, access, or use the application.

The types of information processed depend on how you use the service and

Sombly does not require users to create a traditional public account or
public profile in order to use the chat service.

The application may process information that you provide for the purpose
of operating the service, including:

-   gender;
-   age;
-   chat preferences, including preferred gender and age range of chat
    partners;
-   application settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide and personalize the chat matching
functionality and related application features.

Sombly allows users to communicate directly with other users.

Depending on the features you use, We may process:

-   text messages;
-   images sent through chats;
-   information about when messages are sent or received;
-   conversation-related identifiers;
-   information necessary to deliver messages and maintain chat
    functionality.

Communication data may be processed to operate the service, deliver
messages, support conversation history or favorites where applicable,
investigate abuse reports, enforce Our policies, and comply with legal
obligations.

Users may choose to send images through Sombly.

When you select or send an image, the application may access the

Sombly may also provide an option to save received or sent images to the
device gallery, depending on your settings and device permissions.

We do not claim ownership of images you send through the service.

Users are responsible for ensuring that content they send complies with
applicable law, Our Terms of Use, and Our Child Safety Standards Policy.

Depending on the current implementation of the service, this may

-   conversation identifiers;
-   chat partner identifiers;
-   last activity information;
