At Sombly, we are committed to maintaining a safe communication
environment and preventing any use of the service for Child Sexual Abuse
and Exploitation (CSAE) or Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

This policy describes our standards and procedures for preventing,
reporting, and addressing child safety violations and is intended to
support compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy.

Sombly is an anonymous communication service intended exclusively for
adults aged 18 and over.

## Definitions of CSAE and CSAM

### Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) includes any act, behavior,
communication, or activity that sexually abuses, exploits, or endangers
a child.

This includes, but is not limited to:

-   grooming;
-   sextortion;
-   solicitation of a minor;
-   child sex trafficking;
-   sexual exploitation of minors;
-   attempts to sexualize or prey upon a minor;
-   requesting sexual or intimate material from a minor;
-   facilitating or encouraging the abuse or exploitation of a child.

Any form of CSAE is strictly prohibited on Sombly.

### Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) includes images, videos, digital
media, computer-generated imagery, or other visual material depicting or
sexualizing a minor in a sexually explicit or exploitative context.

CSAM is strictly prohibited on Sombly.

Where technically possible and legally appropriate, detected or reported
CSAM may be removed or restricted, and relevant information may be
reported to appropriate authorities, child protection organizations, or
other legally authorized bodies.

## Zero Tolerance for Child Exploitation

Sombly maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward CSAE and CSAM.

Users must not use Sombly to exploit, abuse, endanger, sexualize, groom,
threaten, coerce, or otherwise harm a child.

Prohibited behavior includes:

-   **Grooming**: Building trust, influence, or an emotional
    relationship with a child for the purpose of sexual exploitation or
    abuse.
-   **Sexualization of minors**: Creating, requesting, sending, sharing,
    or distributing content that sexually depicts or sexualizes minors.
-   **Sextortion**: Using threats, blackmail, pressure, or coercion to
    obtain sexual content, sexual activity, money, or other benefits
    from a child.
-   **Solicitation of minors**: Attempting to contact, persuade, entice,
    or arrange communication with a child for sexual or exploitative
    purposes.
-   **Child trafficking or exploitation**: Any activity connected to
    trafficking, commercial sexual exploitation, or facilitation of
    child abuse.
-   **Inappropriate sexual conduct involving minors**: Sending explicit
    material to a minor, requesting intimate images, engaging a minor in
    sexual conversation, or otherwise involving a minor in sexual
    conduct.
-   **Distribution or promotion of CSAM**: Uploading, sending,
    requesting, distributing, promoting, or facilitating access to CSAM.

Violations may result in immediate restriction or termination of access
to Sombly and, where appropriate, reporting to relevant authorities.

## Age Restriction

Sombly is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older.

The service is not intended for children, and minors are not permitted
to use Sombly.

We do not knowingly permit children to use the service.

If we become aware that a person under 18 has accessed Sombly, we may
take appropriate action, including:

-   restricting or terminating access;
-   removing associated information where technically possible and
    legally appropriate;
-   reviewing related reports or safety concerns;
-   reporting serious child safety concerns to appropriate authorities
    or organizations where required.

## User-Generated Communication

Sombly allows users to communicate with other users.

Users are responsible for the content they send through the service and
must comply with this policy, the Terms of Use, applicable laws, and
other Sombly rules.

Users must never use Sombly to:

-   contact minors for sexual purposes;
-   request sexual or intimate content from minors;
-   distribute or request CSAM;
-   threaten, blackmail, groom, exploit, or coerce a minor;
-   encourage another person to engage in child sexual abuse or
    exploitation.

## Reporting and Blocking

Sombly provides an in-app **"Report and block"** action that allows
users to report inappropriate or abusive behavior and immediately block
the reported user.

Users are encouraged to use **"Report and block"** when they encounter:

-   suspected CSAE or CSAM;
-   grooming;
-   sexual exploitation;
-   solicitation of minors;
-   harassment;
-   threats;
-   abusive or illegal content;
-   other behavior that may endanger a child.

When a user selects **"Report and block"**, the reported user is blocked
from further communication and the report may be reviewed for
appropriate enforcement action based on the available information and
the seriousness of the violation.
